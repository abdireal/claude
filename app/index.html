Turn any item or block into a real 3D model

Import an OBJ, GLB or glTF file (Blender, Blockbench and Sketchfab all export them), fit it into the block, and paint its textures in the Textures tab. It ships in your resource pack.

In game it needs the small BlockGraph Models mod: client side only, Fabric 1.21.11, works with Sodium and Iris. Players without it see the normal item.

You can also drop files here. Pick the .mtl, .bin and texture images together with the model.
